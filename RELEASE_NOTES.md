# Release Notes

App Store text for each release. "What's New" can be up to 4,000 characters and is
tied to a version. Promotional text can be up to 170 characters and can be changed
at any time without a new build.

## Next release: Multiple Buzzers

The project's version is still 1.0. Raise `MARKETING_VERSION` before submitting.

### Promotional text

> Six buzzers, six sounds, one screen. Give every player their own buzzer, pick a sound and color for each, and run game night like a real game show.

Shorter version:

> Now with up to six buzzers at once, each with its own sound and color. Perfect for game night, quiz nights and classrooms.

### What's New

```
Multiple Buzzers

Put up to six buzzers on screen at once. Tap the grid button to choose how many. Each buzzer plays its own sound, so you can set up a whole game, like a buzzer for each player or one each for right, wrong and time's up.

• A sound for each buzzer. Tap the sound name under any buzzer to choose a new one right there. You no longer have to go back and forth to the sound list.
• Your choice of color. Give each buzzer one of six colors, or pick any color you like with Custom….
• Press together. Several buzzers can be pressed at the same time, and each plays its own sound.
• Fits any layout. Buzzers resize to fill the screen in portrait or landscape.
• Remembered. Your buzzers, sounds and colors are saved. If you hide some buzzers and bring them back, they keep their settings.

Your current sound stays on your first buzzer.
```

Shorter version:

```
Now with up to six buzzers on screen, each with its own sound and color. Tap the sound under any buzzer to change it instantly, or pick any custom color.
```
